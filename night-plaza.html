Selected building

You can drag any of the three buildings. To replace artwork later, update the Blockbench model and render a new sprite.

How this works: The models were made in Blockbench, rendered into transparent storefront images from a matching three-quarter angle, and placed inside this plaza. Each building has its own coordinates and can be moved in Design Mode. The glowing city lighting, fountain and sidewalks are part of the scene. This is a separate prototype; the live shopping experience is unchanged until the design is approved.
